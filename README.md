
# Data-Driven Analysis of Wastewater Treatment Performance

## Overview
Analysis of long-term operational data from the
Ismailia Sewage Treatment Plant in Egypt.

## Objectives
- Evaluate treatment performance across three lagoon stages.
- Quantify BOD5, TSS, and coliform removal.
- Examine annual trends and potential performance drivers.
- Explore statistical relationships using regression models.

## Dataset
- Monitoring period: 2000–2008
- Records: 8,211 stage-level observations
- Sampling dates: 2,737
- Treatment stages: 3

## Key Findings
- Mean-concentration removal: BOD5 81.46%
- Mean-concentration removal: TSS 88.57%
- Mean-concentration removal: coliforms 99.75%
- Annual mean final-stage BOD5 increased over the study period.

## Limitations
- Observational dataset; associations do not establish causation.
- The annual trend analysis uses only nine annual means.
- Statistical models show residual serial dependence.
- Regulatory compliance was not assessed.
